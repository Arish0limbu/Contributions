YO MESSAGE
MERO BUDI KO LAGI ❤️
Budi, ma timilai dherai dherai maya garchu ❤️
Timi bina mero din nai adhuro jasto lagcha 🥺
Timi mero lagi ekdam special chau 🥰
Timro muskan dekhera mero mood nai ramro huncha ❤️
Timi sanga bitayeko harek pal mero lagi precious cha 💕
Jati tada bhaye pani mero man sadhai timi sangai huncha ❤️
Malai timro ekdam dherai miss huncha 😘
Timilai herda matra pani mero mukh ma smile aucha 🥰
Timi mero khusi hau, mero maya hau ❤️
Sadhai yestai gari mero sath ma hai budi 💗
Ma harek din timilai ajhai dherai maya garna chahanchu ❤️
Jindagi jasto sukai hos, ma timro sath chhodne chaina 🥺❤️
Timro lagi mero maya kahile pani sakine chaina ♾️
Budi, timi nai mero favorite manche hau 🥰
Dherai maya, dherai miss, ani ekdam thulo ummmaa 😘💋
❤️ 🥰 😘
YO KAHANI KO END CHAINA...
MERO MAYA SANGAI SADHAI ♾️❤️
